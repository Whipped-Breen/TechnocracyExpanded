Uncle Sam In Gray: Technocracy Expanded is a modification based on the technocracy movement form great depression America and Canada which sort to replace politicians and businesspeople with scientists and engineers who had the technical expertise or 'technocrats' as they called them and create a continent spanning superstate called a 'Technate' which would be mostly self-sufficient.

This is an expansion/updating/modification of Rise of Technocracy by TheCrafterESP, who gets a decent chunk of the credit, I decided to take it upon myself too update it because liked it so much (and quarantine of course).
